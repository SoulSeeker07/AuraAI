# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone involved in Aura AI. By participating in this project, you agree to uphold this code of conduct.

## Expected Behavior

### To Create a Positive Environment

- **Be welcoming**: Welcome new contributors regardless of their background, experience, or expertise
- **Be inclusive**: Consider diverse perspectives and experiences
- **Be respectful**: Show respect for others and their contributions
- **Be constructive**: Provide feedback that is helpful and actionable
- **Be patient**: Be understanding of different working styles and communication preferences

### Communication Expectations

- Use inclusive language
- Avoid harassment and discriminatory behavior
- Accept criticism gracefully and constructively
- Engage in respectful debate
- Respect different viewpoints

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, including but not limited to:
  - Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, race, ethnicity, religion, or sexual orientation
  - Offensive jokes or innuendo
  - Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it.

### How to Report

1. **Open an issue** in the GitHub repository
2. **Include**:
   - The date and location of the incident
   - Description of the behavior
   - Names of any people involved
   - Any relevant context

### Reporting Process

- We will review and investigate all reports seriously and promptly
- We will protect the privacy of all parties involved
- We will respond within 48 hours
- We will inform you of the outcome of the investigation
- We will take action as needed to address the behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our code of conduct standards. They may take any of the following actions as they deem necessary:

- Warning the offender
- Requesting a public or private apology
- Temporarily or permanently banning from the project
- Escalating the issue to the broader community
