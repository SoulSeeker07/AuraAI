# AuraAI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

AuraAI respects your privacy.

## Information Collected

Depending on the features you enable, AuraAI may process:

* User prompts
* Conversation history
* Local files that you explicitly select
* Configuration settings
* Diagnostic logs (if enabled)

## Local Processing

AuraAI is designed to support local processing whenever possible. Your files are not uploaded unless you explicitly enable cloud-based features or connect external AI services.

## Third-Party AI Providers

If you configure AuraAI to use third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini, Anthropic, or other supported services), your requests may be transmitted to those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## Data Storage

Conversation history and settings may be stored locally on your device. You are responsible for protecting your device and local data.

## Analytics

AuraAI does not collect analytics or telemetry unless you explicitly enable such features.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to help protect your information. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Responsibilities

You should:

* Protect your API keys and credentials.
* Avoid sharing confidential information with AI services unless you understand the associated risks.
* Review AI-generated content before acting on it.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of AuraAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the copyright owner through the official AuraAI support channels.
